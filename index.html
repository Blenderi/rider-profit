DELIVERY RIDER PROFIT CALCULATOR

Know what you actually
keep from your shift.

Rider Profit is a free delivery rider profit calculator that helps you estimate your real earnings after petrol and riding expenses.

Enter your earnings, completed orders, working time and distance travelled. Your bike's petrol price and mileage are used to estimate fuel cost, so you can see your average earnings per hour, order and kilometre.

You can also add optional maintenance, platform fees and other shift-related expenses when you want a more detailed estimate of your real profit.
